# Performance prestige and verification

Released progression design, 8 October 2026. Existing Bronze–Diamond keys, thresholds and XP stay unchanged. Every performance card adds Pro, Champion, Elite and Unreal, followed by ten unique zero-XP stars. Prestige is revealed after Diamond; Unreal and its star targets are revealed after Elite. Sport mastery retains its existing repeat-session star progression.

Pace stars approach their final target with smaller steps near the ceiling. Benchmarks are fixed snapshots so a later record does not erase an earned achievement. Road-running references use the men’s open road events, not Olympic track records. The half-marathon reference is a reported mark pending ratification. Swimming uses men’s long-course freestyle. These are absolute app benchmark references, not category-adjusted ratings or official record claims. Other pace ceilings are authored app targets because distance, equipment and conditions are not comparable.

Session Builder switches after Diamond to distinct strength days containing at least ten logged sets. Pace Improvement switches to distinct training dates with at least 3% improvement in a 28-day window over the preceding 28 days. These preserve the legacy Diamond requirement and avoid escalating single-session volume or requiring unbounded percentage improvement.

## Verification

The small gold star at a badge’s top indicates that matched, non-manual, non-deleted provider observations support the displayed achievement, including its claimed Unreal star level. This mark is separate from progression stars below the trophy. It carries no XP or ranking benefit.

Verification uses the same canonical observation deduplication as activity verification. Evidence must match the exact log ID and block ID, sport and a non-cancelled, non-future log. Measured source distance/time feed the verified achievement calculation; manually entered better values do not become verified just because a session is linked. Unmatched recordings are not badge proof. Multiple recordings linked to one block cannot duplicate its contribution. Streak verification requires coverage of all counted blocks; a timed strength recording does not verify reps, weights or set totals.

The detail dialog shows verification state, provider names and a collapsed contributing-performance list with direct log-day buttons. The history is recalculated from current logs and source evidence; deleted/revoked evidence removes the gold mark while legitimately claimed trophies remain in history. Evidence is loaded once per performance rewards visit, scoped to the active profile, without blocking badge rendering.

## Validation

1,181 tests across 195 files, the production build, complete badge asset inventory, unchanged legacy threshold/key/XP comparison, dependency audit and diff checks passed. Tests cover faster-is-better targets, distinct star thresholds, ten-star completion, gated mixed-metric prestige, log edits/retries, cancelled and future dates, exact evidence links, slower source values, manual/deleted observations, duplicate contributions, and direct log navigation. Authenticated visual and physical-device checks remain outstanding.
