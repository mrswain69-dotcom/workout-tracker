# Badminton paired avatar release

Sixteen reviewed transparent portraits cover Bronze through Unreal. The same fictional identities develop from junior to mature adult, with playing, walking, leadership, celebration and formal poses. Both appearances have equal kit and award prestige. Full prompts, references and SHA256 records are in badminton-production-record.json. Gold's award received a targeted correction to retain its regional cup rather than the Unreal honour.

Badminton has no legacy collection: ready male artwork is the default until an appearance is chosen. Both appearances share existing logical rewards, thresholds and XP. The additive migration preserves ownership, unlock validation, sanitized group identity and unchanged selection history. Rollback-only SQL checks persistence, permissions and unchanged plan/XP/history.
