# Community discovery and moderation

Program Library → Community has Browse and Saved views, search, sport/difficulty filters and expandable purpose, age range, equipment, duration, creator specialism and verified-creator filters. Sorting supports recent updates, shortest duration and most helpful. Helpful sorting uses a Wilson lower confidence bound, incorporating positive and negative feedback and its sample size; it is not a raw percentage or a programme endorsement. The existing catalogue returns its most recent 200 entries; saved programmes outside that catalogue can still be opened directly.

Programme details offer Save for later, Copy and adapt, Helpful/Not helpful and Report programme. Creator biographies offer Report creator. Bookmarks belong to the selected profile and remain removable when a programme is unpublished or moderated. Saving, copying and voting never apply a plan or award XP. Personal copies appear in My Programs, with the source programme/version retained. Use a copy in Build to adapt its sessions and save a new version. Copies and their direct duplicates cannot be republished as the creator’s own Community work. Existing linked Assessment snapshots survive copying and version edits.

Creators can set Allow personal library copies under programme details → Community publishing. This controls new library copies; existing private copies remain available. Active plans remain editable. The explicit copy RPC checks current permission and version, and unchanged adopted content cannot be saved through the builder to bypass a no-copy choice. As with existing assignment restrictions, this does not attempt to detect rewritten content manually entered by a person.

One helpful/not-helpful vote is allowed per profile per programme, with changing/removing a vote supported. Votes capture the reviewed version and stated purpose; old feedback is excluded after either changes. The server rejects stale-view votes and voting on programmes owned by the same account. Public results show aggregates, never voter identities. Own votes, bookmarks and reports are stored in private tables reached only through owner-checked RPCs.

Reports accept safety, misleading claims, ownership, inappropriate content or other concerns. Reports include a snapshot of the content at submission. A profile can have one open/reviewing report per target and submit at most ten reports a day. Reporter identities, explanations, snapshots and decision notes are visible only in the authorised review queue. No reports or notifications are emailed.

Paul’s nominated creator-review account also has Community review access. The queue is under Community → Review Community reports. Reviewers can mark reviewing, resolve, dismiss, hide or restore publishing permission, with mandatory notes and an append-only decision record. Hiding a creator removes their Community programmes and verified mark. Holds stop normal programme/bio editors from republishing. Restoring leaves content private for its owner to publish again; it does not restore credential verification. Adopted plans and their history remain available.

Library add-on checkpoints now schedule with no assignment foreign key; coach assignment add-ons retain their assignment association. The active plan limit remains one base plan plus one add-on.

Validation includes component tests for saving, permission/PIN checks, voting/removal, copy naming, private reporting, moderator notes, failed requests, stale profile responses, Saved browsing and combined filters. Rollback-only two-account SQL tests cover foreign access, self-voting, copy permissions, private Assessment snapshots, duplicate provenance, feedback revisions, private reports, holds/republication, retained adoption and the add-on limit.
