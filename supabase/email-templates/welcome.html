THE QUICK START

1. Name the athlete.
Open Settings → People.

2. Build the weekly plan.
Add the training that is actually planned.

3. Log the work in the moment.
Use history and targets while the action is happening.

4. Review progress and rewards.
Build a record of improvement, consistency and discipline.

The core idea: see what you did last time, what the target is today and whether you are improving.
